# Source notes

This project uses published aggregate results from the Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2026. It does not use personal or respondent-level data.

## Primary sources

- [AI data](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/ai/data) - trust, usage, validation behavior, and attitude by experience group.
- [Survey methodology](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/methodology) - fielding, inclusion rules, and respondent profile.
- [AI tool usage table](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/ai/data/ai-select) - 17,464 responses to the AI-use question.
- [Trust in AI tools table](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/ai/data/ai-trust) - 14,304 responses to the trust question.

Accessed November 19, 2026.

## Checks performed

- Percentages were copied from Stack Overflow's published tables and checked against the displayed response counts.
- Each chart keeps its own question-level sample size; the project does not treat the full survey sample as the denominator for every question.
- The experience comparison is descriptive. It shows an association in this survey and does not establish that experience causes a more favorable attitude.

## Limits

The survey reports 30,903 valid responses from 169 countries. Recruitment ran through Stack Overflow properties and an opted-in email list, so the results describe survey respondents rather than every developer. Voluntary participation, question nonresponse, and the changing wording of AI questions limit broader or year-over-year conclusions.

The response data is attributed to Stack Exchange Inc., Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2026, licensed under ODbL 1.0.
